Refurbishing old laptops

My school was about to send a batch of old laptops to landfill. I asked if I could take them instead, wiped and repaired what I could, and passed them on — some sold cheaply, some given to people who needed a laptop and couldn't afford one. Mostly about keeping usable hardware out of e-waste.
